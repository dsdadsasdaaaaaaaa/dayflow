Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This policy explains what information I collect when you contact me or book an appointment, how it is used, and how it is protected. I am an independent sole proprietor operating without employees or third-party sales partners.

Information I collect

  • Your name, or whatever name you choose to give me.
  • Your phone number, when you call or text me.
  • Appointment details you provide — dates, times, and location preferences.
  • The content of messages you send me.

I do not run a website form, use analytics, set cookies, or track visitors to this site. Information reaches me only when you send it to me directly.

How I use it

  • To schedule, confirm, and adjust appointments you request.
  • To answer questions you ask me.
  • To keep my own records of bookings and payments.

I do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

Mobile information and text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your phone number and your consent to receive text messages are never sold, rented, shared, or transferred to anyone for any marketing purpose. Text messages are exchanged only between you and me.

Messages are delivered using a licensed communications provider (Twilio), which processes them solely to deliver them. You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for help.

Who else sees your information

Nobody. I do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. I share it only where I am legally required to, or with the communications and payment providers strictly necessary to deliver a message or process a payment you have agreed to.

How long I keep it

I keep appointment and contact records for as long as needed to run my business and meet tax and record-keeping obligations. You can ask me to delete your information at any time and I will do so, except where I am legally required to keep it.

Security

Client records are kept on a passcode-protected, encrypted personal device. Access is limited to me alone.

Your choices

  • Ask me what information I hold about you.
  • Ask me to correct or delete it.
  • Reply STOP to end text messages immediately.

Children

My services are for adults only. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
