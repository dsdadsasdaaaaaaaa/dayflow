About

I am an independent sole proprietor offering scheduled, appointment-based services to individual clients. Appointments are arranged one-to-one and take place either at my location, at the client's location, or at an agreed public meeting place, depending on what the client books.

There is no storefront and no walk-in service. Every appointment is arranged in advance, directly with me.

Booking an appointment

Clients contact me directly to ask about availability. I share my phone number with a client when they ask how to reach me — in person, over a phone call, or in a direct conversation. Clients then text or call that number to ask about times and to book.

I do not advertise the number publicly, and I do not contact people who have not contacted me first.

Text messaging program

Program name: Levi Silverberg — appointment scheduling and customer care.

How you opt in

You receive my business phone number directly from me. You opt in by sending the first text message to that number — for example, to ask about availability or to book an appointment. There is no web form, advertisement, or purchased list, and I never message anyone who has not messaged me first.

What you will receive

Replies to your own messages only: appointment scheduling, confirmations, timing changes, and answers to questions you ask. I do not send marketing or promotional messages of any kind.

Message frequency

Frequency varies and depends entirely on your own activity — typically fewer than 10 messages per conversation.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. I do not charge for messages.

Help and opting out

  • Reply HELP at any time for help and contact information.
  • Reply STOP at any time to cancel. STOP immediately blocks all further messages.
  • Reply START to resume messages after opting out.
